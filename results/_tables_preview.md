*Pooled DerSimonian–Laird random-effects estimates of the hazard ratio per additional syndrome (M2 covariate set), with τ² on the log-hazard scale. Rows 1–3 and the final row are estimated on the same 66,765 participants with a complete exposure and complete M2 covariates and under the same follow-up rule (event detection at the scheduled cognitive waves, follow-up extended to the year of death), so they differ only in the outcome definition. The classification distributed with the harmonized files was available in only 3 cohorts and is therefore estimated on a different, smaller sample (27,425 participants), which is why its k and n differ from the other rows. The first row reproduces the primary analysis in Table 2A exactly. The final row uses the same outcome as the first but censors every participant at their last cognitive assessment instead of extending follow-up to death, so it is a sensitivity analysis on the follow-up rule rather than an alternative outcome, and the two are not duplicates: extending follow-up to the year of death changes the follow-up time of 13,339 of these 66,765 participants (20.0 %), most of them in HRS, which is why the two rows are close but not identical. k is the number of cohorts in which the definition was available and estimable; the criteria differ in sensitivity, which is why the event counts differ between rows.*
